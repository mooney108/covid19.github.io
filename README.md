# covid19.github.io
Covid 19 Management System

Purpose: A browser-based demonstration of a covid-19 management system for medical professionals, patients, administration, the general public, medical supply shops managers, corporate workers, and disinfection workers. 

Features: Find user access by drop down menu; view medical information as patients and medical professional’s update; review medical supplies and shops availability; publish Covid-19 cases in locality; 

How to try it: Open the Pages link. Use the Demo Role menu to switch between medical professionals, patients, administration, the general public, medical supply shops managers, corporate workers, and disinfection workers views. Use the clearly labeled navigation titles on the sidebar of page for direction of use. 

Data and simulations: All patient, supply, and case data are fictional sample data. Sign-in and notifications are simulated; no real health system, hospital record, or messaging service is connected. 

Limitations: This static demonstration does not provide any online communications, updates or real notifications. Data entered is randomly generated and not real.
